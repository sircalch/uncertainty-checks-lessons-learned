\section{Comparison of statistical-inefficiency estimators}

All four estimators were applied to the same production segments. For the MD data, the segments start at the MDCheck equilibration time. Each interval is $\bar x \pm t_{N/g-1}\, s\sqrt{g/N}$, and only $g$ changes between columns. On AR(1) processes (300 series of $N = 20\,000$) and on the OpenMM replicas, the choice of estimator changes coverage by less than 0.01. The shortfall for 100~ps water replicas is therefore common to all estimators that use only the series itself.

\section{FEPCert: reproduction}
The GROMACS analyses are produced by \texttt{validation/benchmark\_alchemtest.py} (alchemlyb 2.5.0, alchemtest, pymbar 4.0.3), the cycle analysis by \texttt{validation/benchmark\_cycles.py} (networkx 3.6), and the figures and tables by \texttt{validation/make\_figures.py}. FEPCert 1.1.0 is loaded from a checkout of commit 83ff97a. In the \textit{short} variant, the first 5\% of the samples of every state are used (at least 20); in the \textit{sparse} and \textit{very sparse} variants every second or fourth state is kept together with the last one. Energies are in kJ\,mol$^{-1}$.
